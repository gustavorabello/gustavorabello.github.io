Profile

Profile

Professor at UFRJ/COPPE working on finite-element methods, computational fluid dynamics, heat transfer and single- and two-phase flows.

Current research covers interface dynamics, complex geometries, microchannel cooling, fluid-structure interaction and high-performance scientific software.

Teaching and supervision span undergraduate, MSc, PhD and postdoctoral work in mechanical engineering and computational science.
