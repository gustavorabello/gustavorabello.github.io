Modeling of last stage of two bubble's coalescence process. The animation shows three different views of the same simulation to illustrate the approaching of two bubbles. Using the 3D ALE-FEM code, we are able to study the physical phenomena that takes place before the unexplored and non-trivial process of bubble coalescence.
